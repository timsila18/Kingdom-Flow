# SolvaHR Employee Self Service Reference

Read-only authorised review: 18 September 2026. Structure only; no credentials, employee identifiers, salary values or personnel records are retained. The session was signed out after inspection.

## Navigation

The employee shell contains My Dashboard, My Leave, My Payslips, My P9 Forms, My Documents, My Messages, My Notifications, My Profile, My Attendance, Issued Documents, Company Documents, My Performance, My Complaints, My Loans & Deductions, My Requests and My Settings.

The shell provides search/jump, notifications, theme selection, install-app, help and sign-out controls. A compact mobile navigation exposes Home, My Work, Payslips, Messages and Profile.

## Directly Observed Screens

- Dashboard: employment information, quick actions, latest-pay summary, shifts, leave, profile completeness, unread notices, loan/deduction summary and recent documents/requests.
- Leave: annual balance, pending approvals, holidays, off-day requests and per-type entitlement/accrued/taken/pending/balance. History columns: event date, leave type, period, days, status, resume and actions. View/download application controls are present.
- Apply Leave: separate full-page form with type, start/end dates, calculated working days and expected resume date. Supporting fields: leave address/contact location, telephone, cell phone, covering person, reason and attachment/document note. Preview: entitlement, available balance, requested days and balance after approval. Cancel returns to leave; submitting was not tested.
- Leave types: annual, sick, maternity, paternity, compassionate, study, unpaid, off-day request and special leave.
- Payslips: latest payslip and history. Columns: period, gross pay, net pay, PAYE, SHIF, NSSF and actions. Explain, view and download controls are present. View was attempted, but document layout was not verified.
- P9: annual-tax-form screen and annual-form list heading. Exported template and issuing rules were not verified.
- Attendance: monthly present, late and absent days; overtime hours; upcoming shifts; recent attendance; overtime/holiday visibility. No request form was inspected here.
- Complaints: category, subject and details, plus complaint history. Categories: workplace conduct, scheduling, harassment, safety, operations and other. Wording assistance controls are present. No complaint was submitted.
- Loans and deductions: salary-advance amount and explanation, active loans, salary-advance history and deduction history. No advance was requested.
- Requests: unified workflow history showing request kind/status, destination approver, submission date, summary, decision note and an Open replies action. Reply submission and approval transitions were not tested.

## Remaining Evidence

Profile amendments, document uploads/publication, messages, notification behaviour, employee performance forms, settings, off-day forms and request replies still need direct inspection. Navigation presence alone is not evidence that their workflows work.

Exact payslip/P9/wagebill/staff-report parity still needs redacted exported documents, including multi-page examples. Do not label the current KingdomFlow annual summary as a P9 or claim PDF-template equivalence from navigation labels.

## KingdomFlow Integration Rules

ESS remains attached to a church member who has a linked staff record; it does not add an eighth church user type. Separate own-staff access from church-administration permissions. Keep private administrative files private unless explicitly published to the employee. Requests require scoped approvals and audit history, not direct changes to payroll or bank instructions.

Paid leave must not exclude a staff member's entire salary. Implement leave policy, working-day calculations, balances, concurrency protection and paid/unpaid treatment before releasing leave-to-payroll integration.
